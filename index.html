How deals are chosen. Every morning the site reads the public deal feeds of DealNews, whose editors check prices before posting. It keeps a deal only when the saving is real: Best deals are 50% off or more (or 40%+ at a lowest-ever price or an editors' pick); Average deals are 25–49% off. Buy-one-get-one counts as 50%, free items as 100%. Credit-card cash back, points, sign-up bonuses and financial offers are left out. Deals stay up for 10 days; prices can change or sell out, so check the price before you buy.
